    Reading the numbers

    The seeds are paired. Run k of every contender uses the same seed, so they face identical starting swarms and identical random streams and the differences that remain are the configurations'. That pairing is what makes the Wilcoxon signed-rank test the right test, and what makes it far more sensitive than an unpaired alternative at these run counts.

    The p-values are approximate below about ten non-tied pairs. They come from the normal approximation to W, and an exact table is needed in that range. The library reports the approximation anyway rather than withholding it, because it is the number a reader can check. Raise Runs each before drawing a conclusion from a marginal p.

    “Double the swarm” is a control, not a contender. It spends twice the evaluations on the same iteration budget, so beating the others is the expected outcome and not evidence about anything. It is included so the rest of the table has a sense of scale.

    Timings under js/wasm are single-threaded and without SIMD. Treat them as relative costs within this page, never as a measure of the library's speed.
